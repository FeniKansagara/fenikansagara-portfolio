Project Overview

This project demonstrates my practical understanding of affiliate marketing through an online fashion store and product promotion campaign.

I created a fashion store named LAVERA, featuring women's clothing and accessories. I also worked on running a product advertising campaign to understand how online advertising can attract potential customers.

Affiliate Fashion Store

LAVERA – Fashion & Accessories

Created an online fashion store with a clean, elegant design focused on women's clothing, accessories and fashion collections.

The store includes product categories, product displays and essential shopping information.
